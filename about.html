Who We Are

About RADILIFE

An electrical engineering and power infrastructure company committed to reliable, efficient and sustainable solutions.

Powering Progress. Connecting Possibilities.

RADILIFE is an electrical engineering and power infrastructure company committed to delivering reliable, efficient and sustainable electrical solutions.

We focus on the development, installation, maintenance and improvement of electrical systems that power homes, businesses, industries and essential infrastructure across Nigeria.

Through technical expertise, professional service and a commitment to quality and safety, we provide solutions that meet our clients’ needs while contributing to a more dependable power infrastructure.

We believe reliable electricity is more than a utility — it is a foundation for productivity, economic growth and improved quality of life.
